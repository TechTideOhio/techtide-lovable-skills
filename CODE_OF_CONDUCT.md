# Code of Conduct

TechTide AI welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on the work, provide actionable feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and publication of private information.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s authenticated GitHub channels and include the relevant discussion or issue link. For security vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
